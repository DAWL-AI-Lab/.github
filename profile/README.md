## About the lab

**Dawl AI Lab** is an applied AI research laboratory within the [Department of Artificial Intelligence](https://www.um.edu.mt/ict/ai/) at the [University of Malta](https://www.um.edu.mt/). Led by [Dr Dylan Seychell](https://www.um.edu.mt/profile/dylanseychell), the lab brings together researchers, students and collaborators to develop and evaluate AI systems in real operational settings.

*Dawl* means **light** in Maltese. For us, it represents both the physical medium behind computer vision, imaging and geospatial sensing, and the clarity needed to understand information, analyse media and promote informed use of AI.

Our work is **research-led, application-driven and rooted in Malta**, with an emphasis on responsible AI and collaboration with public institutions, industry and the wider community.

## Two research pillars

Our work follows the **two pillars defined on our [official website](https://www.um.edu.mt/research/dawl/aboutus/)**:

| **01 / Environment** | **02 / Information** |
| :--- | :--- |
| Applying computer vision, machine learning, drones and geospatial AI to environmental sustainability, mapping, infrastructure and public-service operations. | Applying natural language processing, multimodal AI and explainable methods to media transparency, representation analysis and public AI literacy. |
| **Focus:** environmental monitoring · aerial robotics · geospatial intelligence · urban cleansing | **Focus:** media analysis · information transparency · social equity · AI literacy |

The **Information** pillar encompasses two complementary strands: **Media Analysis** and **AI Literacy**.

## Research and projects

Our projects span applied research, prototype development and validation with real-world partners. The summaries below are introductions; the [University of Malta project directory](https://www.um.edu.mt/research/dawl/projects/) is the primary source for project details and updates.

### Environment

| Project | What we're working on |
| :--- | :--- |
| **AICOM** · AI for Cleansing and Maintenance | Computer vision and machine learning to support urban cleansing, resource prioritisation and operational planning with Malta's Cleansing and Maintenance Division. |
| **AWIGS** · Aerial Waste Identification and Geolocation System | Drone-based litter detection, classification and geolocation to support environmental monitoring and more targeted clean-up operations. |
| **TALC** · Targeted Aerial Litter Collection | Integrating UAVs, adaptable robotic gripping and AI decision-making to retrieve litter from hard-to-access environments. |
| **AAC** · Automated Aerial Capture | AI-guided flight planning and adaptive image capture for more detailed 3D reconstruction of structures and large-scale scenes. |
| **EMBAT** · Enhancing Malta's Basemap with AI Technology | Geospatial analysis of aerial and satellite imagery, including change detection and image-processing techniques for better mapping information. |

### Information / Media Analysis

| Project | What we're working on |
| :--- | :--- |
| **DIMAS** · Data-Informed Media Analysis Suite | Tools for analysing Maltese news content through language processing, sentiment analysis, semantic search and information exploration. |
| **AIRAS** · AI-Driven Media Representation Analysis for Social Equity | Analysis of gender representation and sentiment across Maltese online news to better understand patterns in coverage. |
| **TMMU** · Transparent Media Monitoring and Understanding | Multimodal analysis of broadcast news across video, audio and text to support transparent media monitoring and research. |
| **NBxAI** · Exploring Visual Bias in News Content using Explainable AI | Research into potential visual bias in news using image-text analysis, computer vision and explainable AI. |

### Information / AI Literacy

**NAIL — National AI Literacy Programme** supports accessible AI education and responsible adoption. Developed through collaboration involving the Malta Digital Innovation Authority and the University of Malta, the initiative makes foundational and practical learning available through **[AI Għal Kulħadd / AI4All](https://ai4all.mt/)**.

The programme addresses AI fundamentals, critical and safe use, everyday applications, and learning pathways for different audiences.

## Our expertise

Across these projects, we work at the intersection of:

- **Computer Vision & Geospatial AI** — detection, segmentation, image analysis, mapping and change detection.
- **Autonomous Aerial Systems & Robotics** — UAV sensing, adaptive flight planning and robotic manipulation.
- **Natural Language Processing & Media Analysis** — text and multimodal understanding, sentiment, entities and information retrieval.
- **Explainable AI & Social Equity** — interpretable analysis of media representation, transparency and bias.
- **Science Communication & AI Literacy** — public engagement, accessible education and responsible adoption.

Explore our [research specialisations](https://www.um.edu.mt/research/dawl/specialisation/) for more detail.
